# Contributor covenant code of conduct

## Our peldge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participattion in the Vigilo community a harassmant-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of expreience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes.
- Focusing on what is best not only for us as individuals, but for the overall community.
- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respecting reasonable boundaries and consent in public and private interactions.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without explicit permission.
- Doxxing, stalking, threats, or incitement of violence.
- Deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, or bad-faith engagement.
- Discrimination or exclusion based on personal characteristics.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Vigilo maintainers are resposable for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, pull requests, discussions, or other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct. Maintainers may also temporarily or permanently remove participants from project spaces when their behavior is deemed inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers are expected to respect the privacy and security and security of anyone reporting an incident.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all Vigilo community spaces, including:
- The GitHub repository.
- Issues and pull requests.
- GitHub Discussions.
- Project documentation.
- Chat spaces, mailing lists, and events operated by the project.
- Private communication when an individual is officially representing Vigilo.

## Reporting guidelines

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, oe have any other concerns, please report it privately to the project maintainers.

Use one of the following channels:

1. The contact mathod listed repository or organization profile.
2. A private message to a listed project maintainer.
3. The GitHub reportiing mechanisms available for the relevant content.

Reports should include, when possible:

- Your contact information.
- The names or usernames of the people involved.
- A description of the behavior.
- Links, screenshots, timestamps, or other supporting information.
- Whether there is an immediate safety concern.
- Any request for confidentiality.

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The project will make reasonable efforts to project the privay and security of the reporter and other people involved.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers may take any action they deem appropriate, including:

1. **Correction**: a private or public clarification of expected behavior.
2. **Warning**: a warning with consequences for continued behavior.
3. **Temporary ban**: a temporary ban from project spaces.
4. **Permanent ban**: a permanent ban from project spaces.

Enforcement decisions will consider the impact of the behavior, the context, whether there is a pattern of behavior, and the safety of the community.
